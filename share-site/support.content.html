What to include

  • What you were trying to do and what happened instead.
  • The spot name or shared link when your question concerns a listing.
  • Your app version and a screenshot, if either helps explain the problem.

Never send a password, one-time sign-in code, API key, or private key.

Report a spot

The fastest way to flag a public listing is to open it in the app, choose Report this spot, select the closest reason, and send the report. Reports are private and return the listing to the administrator’s review queue. Use email when you cannot access the in-app report or the issue involves immediate privacy or safety.

Delete your account

Sign in, open Profile, choose Delete account at the bottom, and confirm. This permanently removes the account and its associated spots, photos, favourites, reports, standards acceptance, and identifying moderation history. If deletion cannot finish, try again—the process safely resumes—or email support.
