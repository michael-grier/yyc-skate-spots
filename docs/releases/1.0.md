# Version 1.0 release record

This records the repository's September 7, 2026 submission snapshot. It is historical evidence,
not a live status report. Check App Store Connect for the current submission and account state.

## Submission snapshot

- Production TestFlight build 1.0.0 (5) was attached to iOS version 1.0.
- App Store Connect accepted the metadata and added it to a draft submission on September 7.
  The draft had not been submitted to App Review at that time.
- The version 1.0 privacy answers were entered and published on September 7 after checking the
  archived app's merged privacy manifest. The earlier work was recorded in issues #55, #56,
  and #29.
- Reviewer fields and notes were entered on September 7. Contributor and administrator demo
  accounts were verified against build 1.0.0 (5). Credentials were stored outside the repository.
- Five approved English Canada screenshots were captured on an iPhone 17 from build 1.0.0 (5)
  and uploaded in the order described in [the screenshot guide](../app-store-screenshots.md).

The [listing document](../app-store-listing.md) is maintained separately. Subsequent corrections
to that file, including anonymous sharing, do not imply that App Store Connect was updated.

## Launch-data provenance

The provisioning record described an import of the public development spot set into production.
It discarded development IDs, photos, favourites, reports, deletion state, and moderation history.
Imported spots were published and assigned to the production administrator's Clerk identity.
This describes the initial import, not the current contents of the production database.

Future migrations follow the safeguards in [the environment inventory](../production-environments.md#data-migrations).
